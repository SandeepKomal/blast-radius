# Code of conduct

blast-radius follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
This page summarises it and says how it is applied here.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and respectful, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not the person.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn
  from them.
- Keep the community's interest in mind, not only your own.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as a physical or email
  address, without their explicit permission. That includes personal data
  found in audit logs, screenshots or bug reports: mask it before posting.
- Anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews) and when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/SandeepKomal). Reports are handled promptly and
confidentially. Security problems go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
instead.

## Enforcement

The maintainer decides on consequences, following the Contributor Covenant's
enforcement guidelines: a private correction, a warning, a temporary ban, or
a permanent ban, depending on how serious and how repeated the behaviour is.
